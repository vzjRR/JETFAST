# Reporting Rules

Reports must be factual and reproducible.

## Initial Report

Must describe the system before modification.

## Optimization Report

Must describe:

- what changed
- why
- risk
- verification
- failures
- skipped changes

## Final Report

Must compare:

BEFORE

against

AFTER

Use:

Improved
Unchanged
Regressed
Not Measured

Do not use fabricated percentages.

Do not claim FPS improvement without actual FPS measurements.
